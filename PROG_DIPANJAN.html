Warm-Up Protocol

Complete before every session — same every time.

1. General Warm-Up (5 min)

Rowing Machine or Cross Trainer
5 min
Build intensity over 5 minutes. Start easy, finish warm. Full body activation before lifting.

2. Movement Activation (5 min)

Bodyweight Squats
2 × 10
Full depth. Controlled. Primes quads, glutes and hips before any lower body work.
Arm Circles
2 × 10 each direction
Large controlled circles forwards and backwards. Shoulder mobility before pressing or pulling.

3. Cable Shoulder Series (4.5kg)

Cable Internal Rotation
3 × 12 each side
Elbow tucked at side at 90°. Rotate forearm inward across body. Rotator cuff activation.
Cable External Rotation
3 × 12 each side
Same position, rotate forearm outward away from body. Essential for shoulder joint health.
