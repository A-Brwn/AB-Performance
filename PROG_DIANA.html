Warm-Up Protocol

Complete before every session.

General Warm-Up (5 min)

Cross Trainer
5 min
Easy pace. Raise heart rate gently — warm but not breathless.

Activation (5–7 min)

Bodyweight Squats
2 × 10
Full depth. Controlled descent. Prepares knees and hips.
Band Pull-Aparts
2 × 10
Light band at chest. Pull apart to full extension. Rear delt activation before pressing.
Glute Bridge
1 × 10
Drive hips up and squeeze. Glute activation before lower body work.
Hamstring Stretch
1 × 10
Seated or standing reach toward toes. Hold each rep for 2 seconds.
Quad Stretch
1 × 5 each
Standing, pull heel to glute. Hold a wall for balance.
Arm Circles
2 × 10
Large controlled circles both directions. Shoulder mobility.

Week 1 — Induction weights. Form focus.
