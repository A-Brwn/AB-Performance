Programme Overview

Format: 2 sessions/week · 4-week block
Session 1 (PT): Full body weights — 5 supersets
Session 2 (Independent): 20 min cardio + full body weights — 4 supersets
Equipment: Dumbbells + Barbell only
Note: Lower back friendly — no bent over rows, RDL or squats
