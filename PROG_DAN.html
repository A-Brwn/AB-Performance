Warm-Up Protocol

General (5 min)

Rower or Cross Trainer
5 min
Build intensity. Full body activation before lifting.

Activation

Band Pull-Aparts
2 x 15
Rear delt and rotator cuff before pressing.
Arm Circles
2 x 10 each
Shoulder mobility before upper days.
BW Squat
2 x 10
Prime glutes and quads before leg days.

Strength Day Ramp-Up

Ramp Sets
3-4 sets
On D4 and D5 always ramp up. Example for bench: bar x 10, 60 x 5, 80 x 3, 100 x 1, then working sets. Never go straight to your top weight.
